Telstra Health launches Smart Scribe, an AI-powered clinical note-taking tool in MedicalDirector Clinical that reduces GP admin burdens. Powered by leading AI partners, it automates documentation, saving time and enhancing patient care. Secure and RACGP-compliant, it offers flexible pricing and can save GPs up to $40,000 annually.
