The 2024 Innovation and Technology Across Care (ITAC) event took place on 26-27 March in the Gold Coast, and there were several key themes which underscored the ongoing evolution of aged care towards a more person-centred, digitally driven landscape. Among these were integration and interoperability, empowering and supporting the aged care workforce, and delivering resident-centric care.
